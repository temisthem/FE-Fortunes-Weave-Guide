### A helper app for the game Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave (https://fireemblemwiki.org/wiki/Fire_Emblem:_Fortune%27s_Weave)

Select a character to see every gift they love, or select a gift to see everyone who loves it, along with the Renown and Support levels needed to recruit them on your route.

Pick up to three inn meals to see who loves, likes or dislikes each one.

Select a character to see every Pale Raven remark and the correct reaction for a Perfect Bird Time.

In Extras, browse every recruitable character's requirements, and favourite the ones each lord plans to recruit to build a Recruit Planner (with a shortcut to each planned character's gifts). A setting can hide recruits who aren't in the planner from Gifting, Meals and Pale Raven.

No more flipping between gift lists and recruitment tables!

Spend more time raising supports in Dagsion instead!

Check it out here: https://temisthem.github.io/FE-Fortunes-Weave-Guide/
